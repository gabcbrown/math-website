Talks

An infinite family of non-cyclic 1-cylinder pillowcase-tiled surfaces - Group Actions and Dynamics Seminar, UW Madison, October 2026.

Kra conjectured that Teichmuller space holomorphically retracts to a Teichmuller disk if and only if the quadratic differential generating the disk has only even order zeros. The conjecture is resolved in all cases except half-translation surfaces that are covers of a surface in Q(-1^4) branched over the four poles and with one cylinder in each cylinder direction. To resolve Kra’s conjecture we would like to classify these 1-cylinder pillowcase-tiled surfaces. Apisa-Wright conjectured that they should all have cyclic monodromy. In this talk I will present an algebraic and dynamical characterization of these surfaces that naturally leads to a construction of an infinite family with non-abelian monodromy, complicating the classification picture. Joint work with Malak Abdalla.

Minimality and periodicity in rank 2 interval exchange transformations - Graduate Dynamics Seminar, UW Madison, April 2026.

The decomposition of a translation surface into minimal and periodic components with respect to vertical flow can be investigated through the dynamics of the first return map on an interval transverse to the flow direction. In this talk I will discuss the resulting dynamical system, called an interval exchange transformation, and how in the rank 2 case establishing quantitative bounds on the length of the shortest periodic orbit leads to an algorithm for explicit decomposing a translation surface into cylinders and minimal components.

The J-invariant for Translation Surfaces - Graduate Dynamics Seminar, UW Madison, December 2025.

An infinite family of 1-cylinder pillowcase tiled surfaces - Math For All Conference, Online, April 2025

Kra conjectured that Teichmuller space holomorphically retracts to a Teichmuller disk if and only if the quadratic differential generating the disk has only even order zeros. The conjecture remains open, but work of Markovic-Gekhtman and Apisa-Wright shows that it can be reduced to understanding pillowcase tiled half-translation surfaces with one cylinder in each cylinder direction. We present a new infinite family of 1-cylinder pillowcase tiled surfaces that provide a counterexample to a conjecture of Apisa-Wright, and complicate the picture of classifying geminal orbit closures. Joint work with Malak Abdalla.

What is an Affine Invariant Submanifold? - Graduate Dynamics Seminar, UW Madison, March 2025

This talk introduces affine invariant aubmanifolds/subvarieties, an important tool for understanding GL(2,R) orbit closures in strata of the moduli space of translation surfaces. We'll also introduce the field of definition of an AIS, and provide lots of examples!

Group Determinants and the Origin of Representation Theory - Graduate Algebra and Algebraic Geometry Seminar, UW Madison, April 2024

When Dedekind and Frobenius first discovered representation theory, they were trying to factor something called the group determinant. The goal of this talk is to introduce the group determinant while describing the questions that motivated representation theory. This historical journey will include some Galois theory, lots of character theory, and end with the modern representation theory perspective on the group determinant.
